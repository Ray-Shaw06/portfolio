# Drop a portrait here

Put one photo in this folder and tell me. I will crop, colour-match to the
Lumina palette, optimise it to WebP, and place it in the About section.

What works best here:
- A photo where your face reads clearly at about 400px wide.
- Shot on a plain-ish background, or one I can darken behind you so it sits
  in the dark page without looking pasted on.
- Landscape or square is easier to place than tall portrait.
- Anything you would be happy for a recruiter at a company you want to work
  at to see. This page is the link that goes on applications.

Any format is fine: HEIC, JPEG, PNG. Full resolution preferred, I downscale.
