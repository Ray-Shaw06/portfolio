# Rehaan Shaw portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

SWE and AI engineering recruiters reviewing Summer 2027 internship candidates; engineers who follow a project link and inspect the implementation.

## Product Purpose

Show Rehaan's engineering judgment quickly, then give readers direct access to shipped work, technical write-ups, source, resume, and contact. The first screen must establish his name, UCI CS standing, target roles and term, and the strongest evidence of his work.

## Positioning

Rehaan builds verification around AI output: paid AI coding-agent benchmark and grader authoring, plus a solo product with a deterministic training-plan audit and public red-team page. His broader work spans constraint solving, transfer planning, and a sourced digital exhibition.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is a public, linkable site read on desktop and mobile, often under a short recruiter review window. Case studies are also used as individual links in applications and referrals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, and static export deployment on Vercel.
- Keep the resume, contact, GitHub, LinkedIn, writing, and per-project URLs easy to find.
- Preserve factual claims and traceable project evidence. Do not invent adoption, outcomes, credentials, or benchmark results.
- Employer deliverables are described at a high level; no private task material is published.
- Personal relationship material is outside the portfolio.

## Brand Commitments

Use direct, technical plain English. Lead with the problem, concrete mechanics, a judgment call, and an honest limitation. Avoid inflated adjectives and unsupported totals.

## Evidence on Hand

- `content/projects.ts` and `content/essays.ts` contain case studies and writing.
- `public/shots/` contains screenshots from SpotterAI, Transfer Navigator, Hearth, and Woven Hymns.
- `public/resume.pdf` and `assets/` contain resume source material, though the public PDF needs its current student status checked.
- The life-brain vault and source repos contain supporting facts; project URLs and public source links are in the project records.

## Product Principles

1. Put proof within one click of every claim.
2. Let project artifacts lead the visual experience.
3. Make the site fast to scan before asking readers to go deep.
4. Keep private information and unverified outcomes out of public copy.
